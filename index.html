About Me

I'm Thayaananthan Kanagaraj

AI Engineer · LLM Agents · RAG · LLM Inference

I'm an AI Engineer who builds LLM systems that hold up outside the demo. I recently finished my M.S. in Robotics & AI at Arizona State University (GPA 3.8), and I work as an AI Intern at qBotica in Tempe, where I build the backend for qubi Autopilot, which turns plain-language requests into executable automation workflows. There I replaced an unreliable Computer Use agent with a deterministic, validated pipeline, designed an IP-safe split between an external LLM and a local model, and led a small team on a client-facing voice agent.

Before that, I built a distributed semantic search and RAG system on ASU's Sol supercomputer for a multi-year research study, and fine-tuned a quantized 7B LLM with QLoRA on a single T4 GPU for a medical chatbot at RBG.AI that went to production.

Now I'm going deeper into the layer under the models: GPU programming, kernels and LLM inference performance. I like problems with real constraints, like limited memory, IP and compliance rules, latency budgets, and a client waiting on a deadline.
