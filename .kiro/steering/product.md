# Product: Thayaa's portfolio

Personal portfolio for **Thayaananthan (Thayaa) Kanagaraj**, AI Engineer in Tempe, Arizona.
Live on Vercel (thayaa-portfolio.vercel.app). Repo: github.com/Thayaa21/Portfolio.

## Who reads it
- Recruiters and hiring managers for **AI/ML Engineer** roles, the top target being **AMD** (AI/ML, inference, GPU software).
- Contract/staffing recruiters placing GenAI / AI engineers at enterprises.
- They skim in under a minute, often on a phone. The first screen must say who he is, what he builds, and how to contact him.

## Positioning ("T-shaped")
- Broad: applied LLM engineering (agents, RAG, evaluation), ML fundamentals, Python backends.
- Deep spike (growing): **LLM inference and GPU performance**: CUDA/HIP, C++, kernels. Show it honestly as "currently studying", never as professional experience.

## Look and feel
A mix of three Framer templates:
- **Reflex** (reflex-template.framer.ai) gives the overall **visual style**: near-black violet background, glass cards, soft violet glow, pastel gradient text, a hero "agent console" mock and a glowing arc.
- **Portfon** (portfon.framer.website) gives **portfolio patterns**: a giant faded word behind an arch-shaped portrait, stat cards, and big numbered project cards (01–04) with an "Explore project" pill button.
- **Danie** (danie.framer.website) gives **resume patterns**: mono uppercase section labels in a small box, a vertical-line timeline with a label column on the left and entries in a card on the right, a looping logo/tech marquee, and "BACK TO TOP ↑" in the footer.

It should feel premium and calm. Motion should be smooth, not busy.

2. **No numbers for qBotica work.** Describe what was built, never results or percentages.
3. **No client names** for qBotica work (write "a healthcare client", "an enterprise client").
4. Job titles are fixed: qBotica = **AI Intern**, ASU research = **Graduate Researcher**, RBG.AI = **AI Intern**. Do not "upgrade" them.
5. Do not mention Vian Analytics, startups, "co-founder" or "CTO".
6. GPU/CUDA/HIP/C++ systems work is shown as **learning**, never as experience.
7. Leave out pricing, testimonials, services-for-hire, FAQ, blog and newsletter sections, even though the templates have them.
8. Projects marked `visible: false` in content stay hidden until Thayaa flips the flag.
